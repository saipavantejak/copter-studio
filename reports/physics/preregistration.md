# Frozen public-flight comparison protocol

Commit this protocol and the comparison script before any prediction or metric
calculation. No fitting, parameter sweep, controller training or selection.

Source: https://github.com/idsia-robotics/nanodrone-sysid-benchmark at
`2d921b57d166fe2debe08a5d39bd07297c5abc39`. Use all three files in `data/test`:
`melon_20251017_run1.csv`, `melon_20251017_run2.csv`,
`melon_20251017_run3.csv`. The source describes Crazyflie 2.1 Brushless flights,
not the brushed study aircraft. Do not redistribute the upstream dataset; fetch
the pinned revision for reproduction and record each input SHA-256.

Hypothesis: the implemented held-force rigid-body RK4 subsystem predicts the
logged short-horizon states with finite errors. This is a diagnostic comparison,
not a claim of full-aircraft validation or a pass threshold. Report all flights,
including poor agreement. Do not modify physics or calibrate against these logs.

Use the dataset authors' fixed force conversion from `models/models.py`:
Kt=3.72e-8, Kc=7.74e-12, arm=0.0353; squared motor angular speeds produce
total thrust and roll/pitch/yaw torques in the documented motor order. Use their
`test/test_physics.py` mass 0.045 kg and diagonal inertia
[2.3951e-5, 2.3951e-5, 3.2347e-6] kg m². These are externally provided inputs,
not a fit of Copter Studios. Use production `rk4Step` with zero wind force and
`qNorm` after each step, as in the engine. Keep inputs constant within each
RK4 step; advance to the next recorded motor input for the next step.

Initialize from logged position, velocity, quaternion and body rates at rows
0,50,100,..., provided fifty future rows exist. For each nonoverlapping window,
report predictions at one step (0.01 s) and fifty steps (0.5 s). Require strictly
increasing timestamps with 0.01 s spacing within 1e-7 s; require all selected
inputs/states finite and quaternions nondegenerate. Stop and report malformed
or ambiguous data, rather than silently skipping records or altering alignment.

Metrics: per-flight position-vector RMSE (m), velocity-vector RMSE (m/s),
quaternion geodesic-angle RMSE (rad, invariant to quaternion sign), and
body-rate-vector RMSE (rad/s), separately at each horizon. Across flights report
the equally weighted mean of per-flight RMSEs with descriptive two-sided 95%
percentile bootstrap intervals over the three flight units: ten thousand draws,
Mulberry32 seed 940001 + horizonIndex*10 + metricIndex. Windows are correlated;
do not treat them as independent flights. With three flights, interval coverage
is approximate. There is no significance or controller-robustness claim.

Run the exact comparison command twice with fixed inputs in isolated source
checkouts and diff full generated bytes. Independently recompute all metrics
and intervals from saved predicted/reference states without importing analysis
code. Stop after the registered comparison. No additional horizon, dataset,
parameter or error threshold may be selected after seeing results.

Always retain physicalValidation=false: this protocol bypasses motor lag,
command-to-thrust/BET, the production mixer, air drag, battery, sensor pipeline,
ground contact and flight controller, and uses a different aircraft variant.
Those dynamics, and full free-running flights, remain unvalidated. A good
rigid-body comparison would not validate the residual policy or the full simulator.
